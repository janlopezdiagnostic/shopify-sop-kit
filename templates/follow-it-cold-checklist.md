# Follow-it-cold checklist

An SOP is not tested because someone read it and nodded. It is tested when a
person who did not write it follows it, alone, on a real task.

## Setup

- Tester: an associate who has not done this task from this SOP before, or
  the closest available stand-in.
- The writer watches silently. No helping, no explaining. Every question the
  tester has to ask is a defect in the SOP.
- Use a real task, not a rehearsal, wherever the risk allows.

## During

Note, in order:
- [ ] Every step where the tester hesitated or guessed.
- [ ] Every term the SOP used that the tester did not know.
- [ ] Every place the screen did not match what the SOP described.
- [ ] Every decision the tester had to make that the SOP did not cover.
- [ ] Whether the check steps actually caught, or would have caught, the
  costliest mistake for this task.

## After

- [ ] The task outcome was correct (right items, right quantities, right
  channel, right counts).
- [ ] Every defect found is fixed in the SOP, or listed under "Still to
  confirm" with an owner.
- [ ] The SOP's "Tested" line is filled in: date, who followed it, what
  broke.
- [ ] Anything the tester knew from experience that a new associate would
  not is now written into the SOP itself.

An SOP that has never been followed cold is a draft. Label it that way.
